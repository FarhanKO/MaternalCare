Direction C

Emblem — one bold form, cropped hard

A single flat field, one oversized shape running off the edge, nothing else. No detail to lose when the tile shrinks, and a rhythm of colour down the feed rather than fourteen little illustrations competing with the headlines.

Why it works

Strongest of the three at 86px, because there is nothing small in it. Cheap to extend: a new topic is one shape and one field. And it stays firmly subordinate to the headline, which is what a thumbnail in a reading feed is for.

The tradeoff

Abstraction has a floor: a crescent reads as sleep, but screening and recovery have no obvious emblem and will lean on colour again. Six saturated fields in a column is also a lot of colour next to the dashboard's pale glass.
