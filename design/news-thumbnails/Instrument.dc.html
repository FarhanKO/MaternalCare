Instrument — every tile is a reading

Ink on faint graph paper. The app is built on measurements, so each thumbnail is drawn as the measurement its story is about — a trace, a schedule, a curve.

Why it works

It speaks the dashboard's own language — the same traces, bands and thresholds the vitals charts use two tabs away. Shapes carry the meaning, so the tiles stay distinct in greyscale and for a colour-blind reader.

The tradeoff

Cooler and more clinical than the feed's tone. And a drawn chart can imply it is her data when it is decoration — the shapes must stay clearly generic, or the thumbnail lies.
