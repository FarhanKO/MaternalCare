Why it works

Fourteen distinct pictures instead of five reused motifs. Warm and human, which suits a feed a mother reads in the evening — and flat colour survives being shrunk to 86px far better than a gradient with a thin white glyph on it.

The tradeoff

Every topic needs drawing by hand, so a fifteenth topic is real work rather than a new colour pair. And the illustrative tone sits slightly apart from the clinical restraint of the dashboard next to it.
