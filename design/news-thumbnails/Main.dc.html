The thumbnails as they ship now

Fourteen topics, drawn from one formula: a saturated gradient, a decorative motif at low opacity, and a Lucide glyph dropped in the middle. It works, but it is the same picture fourteen times.

Five motifs, fourteen topics

Rings, wave, bars, dots and arc each do double or triple duty. Sleep and mind are the same picture in different hues; so are exercise and hydration. Scrolling the feed, the thumbnails stop carrying information.

The glyph is doing all the work

A 24px stroke icon centred on a gradient is the stock formula. At 86×74 the icon is the only thing legible, so the motif behind it is decoration paid for in contrast, not meaning.

The circles are ovals

The motif SVG is a 100×100 viewBox with preserveAspectRatio="none" squeezed into an 86×74 box. Every ring and dot is stretched 1.16× wide.

Three directions follow, on the artboards to the right. Each draws the same six topics so they can be compared like for like — and each is shown at the real 86×74 first, because that is the only size that counts.
